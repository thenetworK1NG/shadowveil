01 · First steps

The 90-second loop

Shadowveil is a tight cycle. Open, bind, train, risk, and rebuild. Every card you keep can become a stronger, more valuable version of itself.

Open a pack

Packs cost 100 gold. Finds can be creatures or one-use relics. A fresh profile begins with four starter creatures.

Read your cards

Every owned copy has its own rolled stats, element, level, XP, record, serial, and value. Copies of one creature are not identical.

Build the reserve

Choose three cards for the front line and a fourth Redemption card. No loan cards are added to your battle team.

Risk the arena

Win to earn gold, XP, and an enemy card. Lose and pay the cost. Protect treasures with sleeves before you gamble.

02 · Your hoard

Cards are individuals

No copy is guaranteed

What makes a card unique

  • Randomized stats. Power, Cunning, and Arcana roll separately around the creature template. One copy may be a powerhouse while another is clever or arcane.
  • OVR. The average of the three current stats. Level growth is added on top of the card's own base roll.
  • Element. Each instance carries its own element. It controls the damage edge in the arena.
  • Provenance. Serial, first edition status, grade, wins, losses, streak, XP, and value stay with the instance.

Read the face

The three stat bars are the card's current Power, Cunning, and Arcana. OVR is the quick strength read; the individual bars tell you how that strength is shaped.

Tap an owned card to open its full preview. Tap an enemy card during battle to see its stats, evolution, weak element, strong element, and live matchup numbers.

Collector rule: Do not judge a creature name alone. Compare the actual card in your hand.

Rarity is instance-based

Any creature can roll any rarity. The tier is stored on the individual card, not locked to the creature template. Every copy also receives its own rarity type code.

Common · BRZ Uncommon · SLV Rare · GLD Mythic · DIA Prismatic · PRS Astral · AST Void · VOD Celestial · CEL
03 · The arena

Four cards. One chance.

The enemy builds a response team from the global roster. The battle board shows both decks, the active cards, live element effects, HP, relic statuses, and the next action bar.

Your Coven

VS50/50 opening flip

Enemy Coven

Opening gamble

A true 50/50 coin flip chooses the first side. The result is shown, then tap the screen to begin the match.

Choose a move

Attack, switch to a live card, or spend one relic. The bot gets the same kind of turn after your action.

Redemption reserve

Your fourth card stays face-down. It reveals only after your first three cards are gone, then becomes the final fighter.

Preview everything

Tap your own or enemy cards during the fight for stats and element matchup details without leaving the match.

Redemption scoring

If you win without revealing your Redemption card, all four player cards receive one win and none receive a loss for that match.

If Redemption is used and you still win, it receives a bonus equal to half of the match's player wins, rounded up. A used Redemption card is still a real card: its normal battle events are recorded too.

06 · The long climb

Grow into a god tier

Level cap · 15

Evolution is a visual grind layer over the existing XP loop. A card's random base identity stays intact while levels add controlled growth and unlock a new visual phase.

Ⅰ

Base Evolution

Lv 1–4

The card's original form. Its rolled stats, element, rarity, and art establish the starting identity.

Ⅱ

Second Evolution

Lv 5–9

A new frame and color treatment appear. The same card keeps its identity while level growth raises its current stats.

Ⅲ

God Tier Evolution

Lv 10–15

A special skin, aura, and capped finish mark the card as a long-term champion. Level 15 is the ceiling.

How to add your art: edit creatures/evolutions.json. Each creature has base, second, and god image fields. Leave a field empty to fall back to its normal image. The file accepts a bare filename or a full relative path.

XP and balance

A card earns 100 XP for a win and loses 50 XP for a loss. Each level adds +4 to all three current stats. Wins grow a card; losses can pull it back down. At level 15, XP cannot push it higher.

07 · Protect the rarest

Use a sleeve

Open an owned card preview and choose Sleeve Card. The card gains a visible transparent blue sleeve, shine, and PROTECTED stamp.

Locked from battle

A sleeved card cannot be selected as one of the three battle cards or as the Redemption card.

Locked from selling

Direct sale, bulk sale, and overflow trade paths refuse a sleeved card until you unsleeve it.

Protected crown jewel

The defeat reward cannot take a sleeved card as your crown jewel.

Undo anytime

Unsleeve it from the same preview when you decide it is ready to risk again.

08 · Risk and reward

Gold has teeth

When the legend ends

Gold at zero is not automatically game over. Your cards are runway: their total value counts toward the next 100-gold pack and a fresh four-card battle team.

If you have fewer than four usable cards, no card is still waiting in the lab, and your gold plus total hoard value cannot reach the next pack, the profile is finished.

The game shows Your Legend Ends Here, then resets the profile to a fresh starter hoard.

09 · Condition matters

The Grading Lab

Every card hides a condition grade from 1 to 10. Send a card to the lab for 200 gold, wait 3 minutes, and reveal its condition. Three lab slots can run at once.

Value swing

High grades multiply card value dramatically. A GEM MT 10 is a collector event; a Poor 1 is a hard hit.

Lab lock

A card in grading cannot be sold or selected for battle. It returns when the timer finishes.

10 · The social loop

Trade with intent

Start a trade, share the room code, choose one card each, review both sides, and confirm. The exchange is two-sided: both collectors must confirm before anything moves.

Compare instances

Look at stats, level, evolution, grade, serial, and battle record. A copy's name is only the beginning.

Protect before risk

Sleeve cards you want to keep out of battle, sale, and crown-jewel loss. Unsleeve only when you are ready.

11 · Closing advice

Play the long game

Read the edgeDo not attack on autopilot. The active matchup tells you which side gets the damage advantage.
Save countersShield and freeze are strongest when they erase a charged 2× or 3× hit.
Train specialistsRandomized stats mean one copy may be your best Power card while another is your Arcana card.
Protect legendsSleeve valuable cards before risking a match or leaving them exposed as the crown jewel.
Respect reserveThe fourth card is hidden, but the match is not safe until the enemy's reserve is cleared too.
Evolution is the grindLevel 5 and level 10 are visual milestones. Level 15 is the finish line.
