The First Binding

What a new collector receives and what to do first

✦

A fresh profile begins with 100 coins and four level-1 starter creatures. The starter instances are real cards: they receive their own rolled rarity, element, serial, and stats. They are not temporary loan cards.

The Core Loop

Summon → Bind → Risk → Refine

Spend coins on packs. Inspect the individual cards. Select three front-line cards and one Redemption card. Fight for coins, XP, and a claimed enemy card. Sell, sleeve, grade, favorite, or trade the cards that matter.

  • Summon: packs cost 100 coins and contain five finds.
  • Bind: only creature instances occupy the 15-card hoard; relics live in their own stacks.
  • Risk: a defeat costs coins and removes the current Crown Jewel.
  • Refine: XP, evolution, grading, serials, and battle history change the value of an instance.

Profile and save

Profiles use username and password sign-in. Firebase sync is used when available, with local storage as the fallback. Your collection, relics, pending overflow, grading timers, and battle records belong to the active profile.

Pack Anatomy

The five slots and the laws of overflow

✦

A pack is paid for before it opens. Five independent slots are then resolved. A slot has a 35% relic roll; otherwise it creates a creature instance. If every slot misses relic, the game replaces one random result so that every pack contains at least one relic.

Pack ruleCurrent valueWhat it means
Cost100 coinsCharged before the pack animation
Finds5Five total results per pack
Relic roll35% per slotEach slot rolls separately
Relic guarantee1 minimumA failed pack is corrected to contain one
Creature roster12 templatesCreature pulls choose from the global roster
Relic selection7 typesRelic pulls choose from the relic codex

Overflow

Only creature pulls use the 15-instance creature hoard. Relics do not consume those slots. When a creature pull arrives while the hoard is full, it enters the overflow queue.

Creature overflow

Sell the pull for its calculated value, release it, or exchange it for an owned creature to free a slot. Sleeved or grading-locked cards cannot be used as the replacement.

Relic overflow

Relics stack to three copies per type. A fourth copy does not enter the creature hoard; it converts immediately to coins at that relic's listed price.

Overflow is a decision point, not a second collection page. Inspect serial, rarity, stats, and element before you sacrifice a card for space.

Card Anatomy

How to read an instance, and what makes it valuable

✦

The creature name is only the outer skin. The instance beneath it carries three rolled stats, a level, XP, element, rarity, grade state, serial, first-edition flag, record, and value. That is why two copies of the same creature can have completely different jobs and prices.

Visible combat identity

  • Power: physical strength stat.
  • Cunning: tactical stat.
  • Arcana: magical stat.
  • OVR: the rounded average of the three effective stats.
  • Element: randomized per instance and used by the wheel.

Collector identity

  • Rarity: rolled per instance, not fixed to a creature.
  • Serial: #0001 through #9999 within the creature/rarity print pool.
  • Grade: hidden until the Grading Lab reveals it.
  • Record: wins, losses, streak, and XP affect the instance.
  • Value: drives sales, overflow, Crown Jewel, and Hall of Fame rank.

Stat Rolls and OVR

At creation, each stat is rolled plus or minus ten around its creature template and clamped to 1–100. Each level then adds +4 to all three effective stats; level growth can push a stat above 100.

Stat formula

rolled stat = clamp(round(base stat + random(-10, +10)), 1, 100)
effective stat = rolled stat + (level - 1) × 4
OVR = round((Power + Cunning + Arcana) / 3)

Battle Protocol

Four cards enter the circle; one coven remains

✦

A battle uses three front-line cards and one hidden Redemption card. The enemy has the same four-card structure, but its cards are virtual combatants and are not added to your collection. A 50/50 opening roll decides who takes the first turn.

Your deck · 3 active + 1 hidden Redemption
VS
Enemy deck · 3 active + 1 hidden reserve

The In-Game Board

Upper HUD

The score bar tracks Your Damage against Enemy Damage. The round ticker changes from R1–R4 to KO1–KO4 as cards fall.

Central Focus

Your active card and the enemy active card face each other with HP, level, stats, element, and live matchup numbers visible.

Center Core

YOUR TURN, ENEMY TURN, or CHOOSE REPLACEMENT appears beside VS and the exact element effect.

Relic Rows

Your relics sit above your deck. Enemy relics sit below theirs. A relic fires immediately, is consumed, and ends your turn.

Turn Actions and Damage

ActionResult
AttackStrike the enemy active card.
SwitchMove to another live front-line card; the turn ends without an attack.
RelicUse one relic from your row; its effect resolves and the turn ends.

Normal attack formula

damage = OVR × random(0.85 to 1.15) × element multiplier × pending charge
Damage is capped at the defender's remaining HP. A shield reduces the attack to zero and clears the attacker's pending 2× or 3× charge.

Active HP is calculated as round(60 + OVR × 1.8). When a card is knocked out, the enemy chooses its replacement. Once all three front-line cards are down, Redemption is revealed and activated. The first side to eliminate all four cards wins.

Redemption, Rewards, and the Crown Jewel

  • Clean victory: if Redemption was not used, all four player cards receive one win and +100 XP.
  • Combat-record match: battles involving losses or a used Redemption card record actual combat wins/losses and XP events. A Redemption victory adds bonus wins equal to half the player's combat wins, rounded up.
  • Victory coins: +35 when Redemption was not used; otherwise +10 for every player combat entry with no recorded loss.
  • Defeat coins: -35 per KO event, capped at the coins currently held.
  • Claim: a victory claims the enemy virtual card with the highest OVR, including its level, stats, rarity, element, and serial. A full hoard sends it to overflow.
  • Crown Jewel: on defeat, the enemy removes the most valuable unprotected card in your collection. Sleeved and grading-locked cards are excluded.

Six forces, two multipliers, no neutral guesswork

✦

Every card carries one randomized element. Same-element attacks are even. An advantage deals 1.25× damage; a disadvantage deals 0.8×. The board prints the exact effect before you commit to an attack.

Relic Codex

Seven one-use spells that change a turn

✦

Timing Rules

  • Using any relic is your action; the enemy receives the next turn.
  • Aegis Shard sets the incoming attack to zero and removes its pending charge.
  • Frost Sigil skips the enemy's next turn, giving you the next action again.
  • Shielding or stunning an enemy cancels its pending 2× or 3× charge, but the enemy's charge relic is still consumed.
  • The enemy AI reads element advantage, health, shields, and pending charges before choosing a response.

Current build note

The bot is limited to ten relic uses in a fight. The player-side relic row currently draws from available inventory without enforcing a separate ten-use hard stop; ten is the intended battle-cap rule, but the player limit is not yet enforced in code.

Protection and Favorites

What each safeguard really blocks

✦

A sleeve is a physical ward around a card. A favorite is only a selling preference. They are not interchangeable, and neither one makes a card untouchable in every system.

Favorite rule

Favorites only exclude a card from the Sell Non-Favorites bulk action. A favorite can still be directly sold, traded, graded, battled, or lost as the Crown Jewel.

While a card is in the Grading Lab, it cannot be sold, battled, or selected for trade. Grading temporarily controls the card even if it was sleeved or favorited first.

The Grading Lab

Condition, cost, timer, and value

✦

A card's condition is hidden until submitted to the lab. Each submission costs 200 coins, takes three minutes, and uses one of the three simultaneous lab slots. A completed grade cannot be submitted again.

How to read the odds

The percentages are baseline grade weights, not guaranteed fixed odds. The roll is also influenced by serial range and battle history. A GEM MT 10 is rare, and its 10× multiplier is applied after the grade is revealed.

Lab lock

A card in progress cannot be sold, battled, or selected for trade. It returns when the timer ends. Sleeves do not prevent grading, and a sleeved card remains protected from the Crown Jewel while it is in the lab.

Economy and Reset

The coin flows, bankruptcy threshold, and manual reset

✦

Coins fund packs and grading. Card value is your reserve when the purse is empty. The game does not end simply because the coin counter reaches zero; it checks whether your collection can still recover.

Source or sinkCoin change
Fresh or reset profile100
Open one pack-100
Victory without Redemption+35
Redemption/combat victory+10 per eligible combat entry
Defeat-35 per KO event
Submit a card to grading-200

Bankruptcy Condition

The legend-ending check requires all three conditions:

  • Fewer than four distinct playable creature names remain.
  • No card is currently waiting in the Grading Lab.
  • Coins + total hoard value < 100.

Sleeved cards do not count as playable for the first condition, but their value still counts toward the coin/value threshold. The game shows the legend-ending screen and requires confirmation before creating the fresh starter hoard.

Manual reset

Settings → Clear Account Data → hold for 1.5 seconds. This wipes cards, relics, grades, coins, records, and pending queues, then creates a fresh 100-coin starter hoard. Issued serial reservations are intended to remain reserved.

Trade Circles

A one-for-one exchange between collectors

✦

Trading is an online ritual. Firebase connectivity is required. One collector creates an SV room code; the other joins it. Each side selects one card, reviews the offer, and confirms. Nothing moves until both confirmations are present.

RuleCurrent behavior
Exchange typeOne card for one card; no coin balancing
ConnectionFirebase online connection required
RoomHost creates an SV room code; partner joins
ConfirmationBoth collectors must confirm
Same-name ruleCards with the same creature name cannot be exchanged
TransferStats, level, XP, record, grade, rarity, element, serial, favorite, sleeve, and first-edition status transfer; the receiver gets a new local instance ID

Sleeved cards

A sleeve blocks battle, sale, overflow replacement, and Crown Jewel selection, but it does not block trading.

Graded cards

A completed grade can trade. A card actively in the Grading Lab cannot be selected until the timer ends.

Compare instances, not names. Before confirming, inspect OVR, element, level, grade, serial, record, and value on both sides.

Chapter XII · The Ledger

Hall of Fame

Where the rarest completed instances are ranked

✦

The Hall of Fame is a Firebase-backed global board. Each account publishes its top five graded cards. The public board displays the five highest-valued cards across all accounts, and player-facing entries keep owners anonymous.

Eligibility

Only completed graded cards qualify. Ungraded cards and cards still in the lab cannot appear.

Ranking

Calculated card value decides rank. If values tie, the lower serial number wins the tie.

What builds value

  • Rarity base value and the instance's OVR.
  • Grade multiplier, from 0.2× at PR to 10× at GEM MT.
  • Serial bonus and the additional 1.5× first-edition multiplier.
  • Battle record, including wins and losses.

Collector's target

The statistical ceiling is a specific creature with Celestial rarity, serial #0001, first-edition status, and GEM MT 10. The nominal odds are about 1 in 3 billion: 1/500 Celestial × 1/12 creature × 1/9,999 serial #0001 × 1/50 grade 10, assuming an unused serial and a later grade roll.

Appendix · Quick Reference

Read the Runes

The rules worth checking before you commit

✦

Before a battle

  • Choose 3 front-line cards and 1 Redemption card.
  • Remove sleeved and grading-locked cards from consideration.
  • Check the active element matchup.
  • Keep a shield or stun ready for a charged attack.

Before a sale

  • Inspect serial, first-edition status, rarity, grade, OVR, and record.
  • Favorite a card to skip bulk non-favorite selling.
  • Sleeve a card to block direct sale and Crown Jewel loss.
  • Remember: favorites alone are not protection.

Before grading

  • Cost: 200 coins.
  • Timer: 3 minutes.
  • Slots: 3 at once.
  • The card is locked from battle, sale, and trade during the timer.

Before trading

  • Confirm Firebase connectivity.
  • It is one card for one card; coins cannot balance the offer.
  • Same-creature names are rejected.
  • Both collectors must confirm.

Four numbers to remember

100 coins per pack · 5 finds per pack · 15 creature hoard slots · 15 maximum card level

When in doubt, inspect the instance. The game is built around the difference between a creature template and the particular card in your collection.
